SnoopCam Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026. This privacy policy explains how SnoopCam Inc. collects, uses and shares personal information.

Information we collect

We collect video and audio recordings captured by your device, including recordings of visitors and passers-by. We collect facial geometry ("Familiar Faces") to recognize people, device location, Wi-Fi network information, contacts you invite, and usage data. We may collect information about you from third parties, including data brokers and advertising partners.

How we use and share information

We may share recordings with law enforcement upon request, with or without a warrant, where we believe it is appropriate. We share personal information with our affiliates, advertising partners and other third parties for marketing. We may sell de-identified and aggregated data. We use recordings and facial data to train our machine learning models and improve AI features.

Retention

We retain recordings and biometric data for as long as necessary for our business purposes.

Your choices

You may request deletion by mailing a letter to our headquarters. Some data cannot be deleted.
